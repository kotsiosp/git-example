# Registering with GeSy (General Healthcare System / ΓεΣΥ)

GeSy is Cyprus's General Healthcare System. Beneficiaries register with a Personal
Doctor (GP) and then access healthcare through the system. Registration is done online
through the GeSy beneficiary portal or with the help of a Personal Doctor's office.

## Who can register

Beneficiaries generally include Cypriot citizens, EU citizens residing in Cyprus, and
other categories of legal residents who contribute to the system. (Verify eligibility
for your specific residence status on the GeSy pages.)

## How to register

1. Obtain the credentials needed for the beneficiary portal (for example, government
   digital-authentication credentials or the details requested by GeSy).
2. Log in to the GeSy beneficiary portal.
3. Choose a Personal Doctor (GP) for adults, or a Personal Doctor (paediatrician or GP)
   for children, from the list of available doctors accepting new beneficiaries.
4. Confirm your registration. You can change your Personal Doctor later, subject to the
   system's rules on how often changes are allowed.

## Contributions

GeSy is funded by contributions calculated as a percentage of income, withheld or paid
by employees, employers, the self-employed, pensioners, and others. The exact
contribution rates and the income ceiling change over time — confirm the current rates
on the GeSy or Social Insurance pages.

## Using GeSy

After registering, you access your Personal Doctor first; they refer you to specialists,
labs, and hospitals within the system as needed. Keep your beneficiary details up to
date if your circumstances change.
