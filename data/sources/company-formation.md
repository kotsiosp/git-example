# Forming a Private Company Limited by Shares

New companies in Cyprus are registered with the Department of Registrar of Companies and
Intellectual Property. The most common vehicle is a private company limited by shares.

## Step 1 — Approve the company name

Before incorporation, submit the proposed company name for approval to the Registrar. A
name is rejected if it is identical or too similar to an existing name, or if it is
considered misleading or undesirable. Approved names are reserved for a limited period.

## Step 2 — Prepare incorporation documents

Typical documents include:

- Memorandum and Articles of Association, signed and (where required) certified.
- Details of the registered office address in Cyprus.
- Details of directors, secretary, and shareholders.
- The declaration/forms prescribed by the Registrar for incorporation.

Many applicants use a lawyer or licensed service provider to prepare and certify these
documents. (Verify the exact current document set and any certification requirements on
the Registrar's site.)

## Step 3 — Submit and pay fees

Submit the incorporation application (online where available) and pay the incorporation
fees, including any capital-related fee. On approval, the Registrar issues the
Certificate of Incorporation and related certificates (directors/secretary,
shareholders, registered office).

## After incorporation

- Register with the Tax Department for a Tax Identification Code, and for VAT if
  required.
- Register as an employer with Social Insurance if you will hire staff.
- File annual returns (form HE32) and financial statements with the Registrar, and keep
  statutory registers up to date.

Fees, forms, and timelines change — confirm current requirements with the Registrar of
Companies before filing.
