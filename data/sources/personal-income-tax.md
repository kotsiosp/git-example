# Personal Income Tax Return (form TD1)

Individuals who are tax residents of Cyprus, and certain non-residents with
Cyprus-source income, may be required to submit an annual personal income tax return
(form **TD1**) through the TAXISnet online system operated by the Tax Department.

## Who must file

Filing obligations depend on your total income and sources. Employees, self-employed
persons, pensioners, and people with rental or investment income may all have to file.
Some low-income individuals are exempt. (Verify the current filing threshold and
exemptions for the relevant tax year on the Tax Department pages.)

## How to file

1. Register for a TAXISnet account (Tax Identification Code / TIC required).
2. Log in and open the TD1 return for the relevant tax year.
3. Declare income (employment, self-employment, pensions, rents, dividends, interest)
   and any allowable deductions (e.g. social insurance and GeSy contributions, life
   insurance premiums, and donations, subject to limits).
4. Submit electronically and pay any balance of tax due.

## Deadlines

Personal income tax returns are filed after the end of the tax year, which is the
calendar year. Deadlines are set annually and differ for employees versus self-employed
persons who prepare audited accounts. (Verify the exact submission and payment dates for
the current year — do not rely on a remembered date, as deadlines are sometimes
extended by decree.)

## Tax bands

Cyprus applies progressive income tax bands with a tax-free portion followed by rising
rates. The exact band thresholds and rates change over time — confirm the current bands
on the Tax Department site rather than assuming previous years' figures.
