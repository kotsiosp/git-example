# Yellow Slip (Registration Certificate, form MEU1)

The "Yellow Slip" is the Registration Certificate issued to EU/EEA/Swiss citizens who
intend to reside in the Republic of Cyprus for longer than three months. It is applied
for at the Civil Registry and Migration Department (CRMD) or through a District
Administration office. The application form is **MEU1**.

## Who needs it

EU, EEA, and Swiss nationals staying more than three months. Non-EU nationals follow a
different process (Pink Slip / temporary residence permit) and should not use MEU1.

## Documents usually required

- A completed MEU1 form.
- Valid passport or national ID card (original and a copy).
- Proof of the reason for residence: an employment contract or employer confirmation
  (for workers); proof of registration and sufficient resources (for students); or
  proof of sufficient financial resources and health insurance (for self-sufficient
  persons).
- Proof of address in Cyprus, such as a rental agreement or utility bill.
- Passport-size photographs.

Bring originals plus copies. Documents in a foreign language may need an official
translation. (Verify the current exact checklist on the CRMD pages before you go.)

## Process

1. Book an appointment or attend the CRMD / District Administration office that serves
   your district (for example Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, Famagusta).
2. Submit the MEU1 form with the supporting documents and pay the registration fee
   (verify the current fee — historically a small fixed amount).
3. The Registration Certificate (Yellow Slip) is issued once the application is
   approved.

## Notes

- The Yellow Slip does not expire in the way a visa does, but you should update your
  details if your circumstances change.
- Family members who are themselves non-EU nationals use a separate family-member form
  and process.
