# VAT Registration in Cyprus

Value Added Tax (VAT) is administered by the Tax Department. Businesses and
self-employed persons must register for VAT when they meet the conditions set out in the
VAT legislation.

## When registration is required

Registration is generally required when taxable turnover exceeds the registration
threshold over a rolling period, or is expected to exceed it in the near future. There
are also rules for:

- Businesses making intra-EU acquisitions above a threshold.
- Providers of certain services to, or recipients of services from, other EU or
  non-EU businesses (reverse charge).
- Distance sellers and providers of digital services.

Voluntary registration below the threshold is possible in some cases. (Verify the
current registration threshold and the specific triggers on the Tax Department pages.)

## How to register

1. Register through the Tax Department's online services (Tax For All / TAXISnet, as
   applicable) or submit the prescribed VAT registration form.
2. Provide business details, activity description, and expected or actual turnover.
3. Once registered, you receive a VAT number and must charge VAT at the correct rate,
   issue compliant invoices, and file periodic VAT returns.

## Ongoing obligations

- File VAT returns for each VAT period by the due date and pay any VAT owed.
- Keep proper records and issue valid VAT invoices.
- Deregister if you cease trading or fall below the deregistration conditions.

The standard and reduced VAT rates, thresholds, and filing periods change over time —
confirm the current figures with the Tax Department before relying on them.
